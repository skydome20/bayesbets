Methodology

Our model uses a three-component weighted Bayesian framework (λ_prior + λ_obs + λ_recent) anchored to xtracker timestamp data. Each report updates the posterior with all tweet activity observed up to that timepoint.

Why closer = better: At T-12h, the model relies primarily on the prior (historical patterns). By T-1h, the vast majority of data has been observed, and λ_recent — the single most predictive variable — carries 50% weight, dramatically tightening the posterior predictive distribution.

Each report includes the full posterior predictive distribution across all Polymarket brackets, a bi-directional EV matrix, position sizing recommendations, and risk monitoring thresholds with trigger actions.
